Welcome to the Ranch

A charming ranch built for the day you'll never forget.

Honey Acres is a charming ranch and wedding venue in DeLand, Florida. Featuring stunning views, a complementing reception area, and a magnificent oak tree for your vows.

We take great pride in the inviting atmosphere we've crafted for our guests to celebrate their perfect day. Our venue offers all the essentials to ensure your special day is unforgettable — and with the support of our owners, Sherry and David, you have all the tools for an exceptional experience.
